# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short form is below; the linked text is the version we mean.

## What we expect

- Be direct about the work and kind to the person. Review a change, not a person.
- Assume a reader who is not in the conversation. Explain the reason, not only the decision.
- Accept a "no" that comes with a reason. Disagreement is settled by evidence, not by volume.
- Do not post anyone's private information, credentials, or checkpoint data.

Behavior that is unwelcome here: harassment, insults, sexualized attention, deliberate
misattribution, and repeated disruption of a thread after a moderator has asked it to stop.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any space where someone is
speaking for the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer (`@bolubo`) through their GitHub profile, or with GitHub's report form if
you prefer not to contact them directly. A report is handled privately; the reporter is not named
to the person reported without their consent.

Responses, in order of severity: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block from the
repository's conversations, a permanent block. The maintainer decides, and the decision is recorded
in the issue thread with the least detail that still explains it.
